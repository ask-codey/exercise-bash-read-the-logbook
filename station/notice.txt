GULL ROCK LIGHT STATION
Notice to the new keeper

Welcome to Gull Rock. The station's logbook is in logbook.txt.
Every keeper writes a few lines a day: the weather, when the lamp
was lit, and anything unusual out on the water.

The logbook is long, so don't try to read it with cat. Open it
with less, and search it with / when you're looking for something.

The supplies folder has the orders and the charts. One of those
files isn't text, whatever its name says, so check before you open it.

Good luck, and keep the light burning.

- Ruth Halloran, keeper
